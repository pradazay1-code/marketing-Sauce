## 1. Executive Summary

AventisAI is a rebranded white-label software product that packages CRM, marketing automation, and AI tools under the Aventis brand and sells them as a monthly subscription to small-to-mid businesses (5-50 employees). Instead of doing hands-on marketing work for each client, Aventis sells the *software* — creating recurring revenue with 80-90% margins and minimal ongoing labor.

**The core idea:** Pay ~$497/mo for an unlimited white-label platform, rebrand it as AventisAI, sell subscriptions at $297-$997/mo per client. At 10 clients, you're making $3,000-$10,000/mo profit with almost no marginal cost per new customer.

## 14. Sales Playbook — How to Gain & Close

### The 4-Step Close (Hormozi CLOSER Framework)

**C — Clarify** their current situation
```
"How are you currently handling [CRM/follow-ups/reviews/marketing]?"
"How many different tools are you paying for?"
"What's that costing you per month?"
```

**L — Label** the problem
```
"So you're spending $800/mo on 6 different tools that don't talk to each other,
you're losing leads because nobody follows up fast enough, and your reviews
are going unanswered. Does that sound right?"
```

**O — Overview** your solution
```
"What if I could replace all 6 tools with one platform, automate your
follow-ups so no lead ever falls through the cracks, and have AI respond
to every review — for less than what you're paying now?"
```

**S — Sell** the value, not the price
```
"The businesses using this are saving 10-15 hours a week and getting
30-40% more leads just from the automated follow-ups alone.
The platform is $497/mo — less than what you're spending on
Mailchimp and Calendly combined."
```

**E — Explain** what happens next
```
"Here's what happens when you sign up: I set everything up for you
within 48 hours. Import your contacts, build your pipeline, configure
your automations. You'll have a live AI chatbot on your website by Friday.
All you do is say yes."
```

**R — Reinforce** with the guarantee
```
"And if you don't see the value in 30 days, I'll refund you completely.
There's literally zero risk."
```
