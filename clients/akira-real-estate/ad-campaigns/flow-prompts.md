# PART 1 — THE 6-FRAME AD SET (IMAGES)

Designed as an Instagram carousel. Each frame earns the swipe to the next.
Angle: seller / home valuation — the highest-value lead type.

## Locked style block — append to every prompt

```
Shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field, fine film grain. Deep navy and warm amber
palette, muted premium editorial grade, soft directional warm light from the left.
Cinematic high-end real estate brand aesthetic. No text, no logos, no faces, no
people. 4:5 vertical.
```

Generate Frame 1 first, then **reuse its seed number** on every following frame.
That is what locks the look. Without it you get six different photographers.

## Overlay specs — keep identical across all frames

| Element | Setting |
|---|---|
| Font | Clean geometric sans — Inter, Poppins, or Neue Haas |
| Headline | White, 60–72px, bold, left-aligned |
| Body | White at 80% opacity, 28–32px regular |
| Placement | Same corner every frame. Pick one, never move it. |
| Safe margin | 120px from every edge |
| Logo | Small, bottom-left, frames 1 and 6 only |

Never center headline text over a busy area. Every prompt specifies where the
negative space sits — put the text there.

## How to run it

- **Carousel:** post all six. Swipes are weighted heavily by the algorithm.
- **Reel:** same six frames, 1.5s each, subtle push-in, one trending audio track.
- **Paid ad:** frames 1, 3, and 6 only. Cold traffic will not swipe six times.
- **Hashtags:** organic only, first comment. None on paid.

# PART 2 — THE 6-FRAME AD SET (VIDEO / VEO)

Same narrative arc as above, built as motion. Every parameter broken out so any
single variable can be edited without rewriting the prompt.

## Global style block — paste at the end of every scene

```
STYLE: Cinematic real estate brand film. Deep navy and warm amber palette. Muted
premium editorial color grade, lifted blacks, gentle highlight rolloff. Fine 35mm
film grain, subtle halation on highlights. Anamorphic character without heavy distortion.
NEGATIVE: no text, no watermarks, no logos, no signage lettering, no visible faces, no
people in frame, no warped architecture, no duplicated windows, no bending straight
lines, no fisheye, no oversaturation, no HDR clipping.
FORMAT: 4:5 vertical, 24fps, ~8 seconds.
```

Generate Scene 1 first. Save the door and the house exterior as **Ingredients**,
then carry them into later scenes.

---

### SCENE 1 — The Hook
```
SHOT: Medium close-up, eye level, centered.
CAMERA: Very slow dolly push forward, roughly 18 inches of travel across the full clip.
No handheld shake. Smooth motorized slider feel.
LENS: 85mm, f/2.0, shallow depth of field.

SUBJECT: A classic six-panel front door painted deep navy, polished unlacquered brass
knob and knocker catching light. Door is closed. Weathered brass slightly patinated,
not showroom new.

SETTING: Covered front porch of an older New England home. Painted white trim, aged red
brick step in the lower foreground, a trimmed boxwood partially entering the left edge,
softly out of focus.

LIGHTING: Single warm porch sconce above frame, roughly 2700K, casting a soft downward
pool across the upper door panels. Ambient dusk fill from camera left at 5600K. Strong
contrast between warm key and cool ambient. Brass hardware carries a small specular
highlight.

ATMOSPHERE: Late blue-hour dusk. Faint cool haze in the background falloff.
MOTION IN SCENE: Nothing moves except the camera. Absolute stillness. One boxwood leaf
drifts slightly in a light breeze.
COMPOSITION NOTE: Leave the upper third in soft shadow and negative space for text.
AUDIO: Quiet residential evening. Distant crickets low in the mix. One faint car passing
several streets away. No music.
```
**Overlay:** Your home is worth more than you think.

### SCENE 2 — The Tension
```
SHOT: Wide interior, low camera height, roughly 3 feet off the floor.
CAMERA: Slow lateral truck moving right to left, parallel to the back wall, approximately
4 feet of travel. Constant speed, no acceleration. Locked horizon.
LENS: 28mm, f/4, moderately deep focus.

SUBJECT: A completely empty living room. Wide plank white oak flooring with visible grain.
Bare eggshell walls. One tall double-hung window on the right wall.

SETTING: Older home interior, freshly cleared. Simple painted baseboard trim. No furniture,

picture once hung.

LIGHTING: Hard late-afternoon sunlight entering through the window at a steep angle, throwing

boards as the camera trucks. Deep soft shadow filling the room's left side.

ATMOSPHERE: Fine dust motes drifting slowly through the sunbeam, clearly visible against the
shadow behind them.
MOTION IN SCENE: Only dust in the light. Total stillness otherwise.
COMPOSITION NOTE: Keep the upper left quadrant in shadow for text placement.
AUDIO: Hollow room tone with faint natural reverb. Muffled birdsong through glass. Very
distant lawnmower. Nothing else.
```
**Overlay:** Most Massachusetts homeowners have no idea what they're sitting on.

SHOT: Macro product still life, camera at object eye level.

ATMOSPHERE: Clean studio air. No haze, no particles.
MOTION IN SCENE: Only the camera and the traveling shadow.
COMPOSITION NOTE: Model sits in the lower third. Upper two thirds is pure empty navy for a

AUDIO: Deep low ambient studio hum. One subtle rising synth tone swelling gently across the

## SceneBuilder assembly

| | |
|---|---|
| Order | 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 |
| Transitions | Straight cuts between 1–4. Half-second dissolve into 5 and into 6. |
| Runtime | ~48 seconds. Trim to 30 by cutting Scene 5 for paid. |
| Text timing | In at 0.8s after each cut, out 0.5s before the next |
| Music | One continuous bed under all six so cuts feel like one film |

Camera-move variety is deliberate: push in → truck across → orbit → crane up →
descend → boom out. Six identical push-ins would read as generated.

## Troubleshooting — edit one line, regenerate

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Architecture warping, windows multiplying | Strengthen NEGATIVE, move to a longer lens |
| Too bright or flat | State key light direction and color temperature more forcefully |
| Camera drifting or shaky | Add "locked horizon, motorized slider, no handheld motion" |
| Too much happening | MOTION IN SCENE is the control — state what stays still |
| Wrong mood | Adjust COLOR and ATMOSPHERE only, leave the rest alone |

## Universal negative prompt

```
--no text, watermark, logo, people's faces, distorted architecture, extra windows, warped straight lines
```

AI is notably bad at architectural geometry — windows multiply, rooflines bend. The
negative prompt catches most of it.

# STANDING RULE

**None of this depicts a property Kunal has listed, and that is deliberate.**

Everything here is atmosphere, emotion, or abstract. Passing AI-generated imagery off
as real inventory runs into deceptive advertising rules, state licensing standards, and
MLS policy — the exposure is materially higher in real estate than in most categories.

Use these as hooks, B-roll, and backdrops. Use real photography for anything that
represents an actual home.

Worth confirming Kunal's brokerage AI-content policy before the first post runs.
