## Market Context (2026)

Current MA real estate landscape shaping this campaign:
- **Mortgage rates** stabilizing in the mid-6% range after peaking in 2023-24 — buyers who waited are now re-entering
- **Home equity at record highs** — average MA homeowner sitting on $180K+ in equity, ripe for seller conversion
- **Inventory still tight** — 2.1 months supply in most MA markets (seller's market continues)
- **Millennials** now peak home-buying age (32-42) with growing families needing more space
- **AI-driven home search** shifting how buyers discover properties — meet them where they are

**Strategic angles:** Urgency (rates dropping = buyer competition returning), equity unlock for sellers, education for first-time buyers, hyper-local authority.

## Universal Ad Setup Guide

### Campaign Structure in Meta Ads Manager
```
Campaign Level: Choose objective (Leads for #1-3, Traffic for #5, Awareness for #4)
  └── Ad Set Level: Set audience, budget, placement
       └── Ad Level: 3-5 variations per ad set for testing
```

### Placement Recommendations
- **Enable:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels
- **Disable:** Audience Network (low quality traffic), Right Column (low performance)
- **Advantage+ Placements:** Turn ON for broader reach at lower cost

### Audience Layering (Real Estate Best Practices)
| Layer | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Location** | MA + 25 mile radius from target zip codes |
| **Age** | 28-65 (adjust by campaign) |
| **Interests** | Real estate, mortgage, home buying, Zillow, Realtor.com, home improvement, Wayfair |
| **Behaviors** | Likely to move, recently engaged, new parents, small business owners |
| **Exclusions** | Real estate agents, licensed brokers (they don't convert) |

### Retargeting Sequence
Set up custom audiences for:
1. **Website visitors** (last 30/60/90 days) — hit with Campaign 4 (Just Sold)
2. **Video viewers** (75%+ of any video) — hit with Campaign 1 or 2
3. **Lead form openers who didn't submit** — hit with a simplified follow-up ad
4. **Instagram profile visitors** — hit with Campaign 5 (Neighborhood)

### Recommended Budget Allocation ($60/day total)
- Campaign 1 (Seller Leads): $20/day — highest revenue potential
- Campaign 2 (Buyer Leads): $20/day — steady lead flow
- Campaign 3 (First-Time Buyer): $10/day — long-term nurture
- Campaign 4 (Just Sold): $5/day — always-on social proof
- Campaign 5 (Neighborhood): $5/day — organic reach amplifier

### KPIs to Track Weekly
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Cost per Lead (Seller) | Under $35 |
| Cost per Lead (Buyer) | Under $20 |
| Click-Through Rate | Above 2% |
| Frequency | Under 2.5 (refresh creative if higher) |
| Video Hook Rate (3-sec view) | Above 25% |

## Compliance Notes (Fair Housing)

All ads MUST comply with Fair Housing Act:
- **Never** target by race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability
- Meta automatically restricts detailed targeting for housing ads (this is expected)
- Include Equal Housing Opportunity disclaimer in ad or landing page
- Avoid language like "safe neighborhood," "family-friendly," "walking distance to church"
- OK: "spacious," "updated," "great value," "quiet," "convenient location"
