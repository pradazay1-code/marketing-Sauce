## Sequencing

Don't post all five at once. Run them like this:

| Week | Ad | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ad 1 (5 Reasons) | Establish credibility first |
| 2 | Ad 3 (Sell in 2026) | Seller leads — run paid |
| 3 | Ad 2 (Before/After) | Buyer leads — run paid |
| 4 | Ad 5 (Skip the Noise) | Softer buyer entry |
| 5 | Ad 4 (Move vs Wait) | Retarget anyone who engaged but didn't convert |

Ad 4 works best as **retargeting** rather than cold — it's aimed at people already thinking about it, which is exactly who your pixel has collected by week 5.

---

**One thing to check:** Ad 4 has a testimonial attributed to "Lena & Mark, Somerville." If that's a real client quote with their permission, great — it's the strongest element in the whole set. If it came from the AI generation, pull it before this runs. Fabricated testimonials are deceptive advertising, and real estate licensing boards treat that seriously.

Easy fix either way: swap in a real review from Kunal's Google or Zillow profile, or remove the quote block.
