## Section 2 — Facebook Content Strategy (15 min)

### The Big Idea
**Post 5x per week on Facebook.** Consistent presence beats sporadic posting. Every post should hit ONE of these goals:

1. **Educate** — teach something valuable about buying/selling in MA
2. **Show authority** — prove you know the market
3. **Build trust** — show your personality and process
4. **Drive leads** — direct CTA to book a consultation

## Section 8 — Objection Handling (Prepare For These)

### "That's a lot of money"
**Response:** "One extra deal per month pays for the entire year. If your average commission is $12K and we get you 5-10 extra deals annually, that's $60K-$120K in revenue. The ROI is 20-40x."

### "I want to see results before I commit long-term"
**Response:** "That's why we do month-to-month, no contracts. If you don't see leads in the first 30 days, cancel anytime. We stand behind our work with a 30-day money-back guarantee."

### "Can't I just do this myself?"
**Response:** "Absolutely — but every hour you spend on marketing is an hour you're not on showings or closings. What's your hourly rate as a realtor? If it's more than what we charge, hiring us is a no-brainer."

### "Facebook doesn't work anymore"
**Response:** "It doesn't work for people posting random content. It works incredibly well when you have a targeted strategy, professional creative, and consistent execution. We do all three."

### "Let me think about it"
**Response:** "Of course — real estate is your business, so you should think through it. But every week you wait, you're missing 20-30 potential leads. Can we book a follow-up for [3 days from now]?"
