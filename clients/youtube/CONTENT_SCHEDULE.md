# Mindbloom 30-Day Content Schedule

60 pre-rendered videos. 2 posts per day. Schedule on TikTok + YouTube Shorts using each platform's native scheduler.

**Posting times (Eastern):**
- 🌞 AM slot: **12:00 PM ET** (lunch scroll window)

**Cross-post sequence:** Post on TikTok 5 min before YouTube Shorts. That way TikTok's faster algorithm gives you early signal before Shorts' slower start.

**Day 1 = your launch day** (e.g. if you start Monday, Day 1 = Monday, Day 7 = Sunday, etc.)

Voice: Edge TTS (free) — add `"use_elevenlabs": true` to any JSON to upgrade that video to ElevenLabs Adam.

## How to schedule on each platform

### YouTube Shorts (web)
1. https://studio.youtube.com → **Create** → **Upload video**
2. Drop the MP4 → fill in title/description from your captions doc
3. Visibility → **Schedule** → pick date + time
4. Repeat for each video. You can schedule unlimited videos ahead.

### TikTok (desktop web only)
1. https://www.tiktok.com/upload?lang=en
2. Drop the MP4 → fill in caption
3. Toggle **"Schedule"** → pick date + time (up to **10 days ahead**)
4. Since TikTok caps at 10 days, you'll batch upload weekly (every Sunday, schedule the next 10 days)
